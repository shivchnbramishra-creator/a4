Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Alligator Luxe guide.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Readers rely on Alligator Luxe for clear, accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment, free products or gifts in exchange for coverage, and our guides are brand-neutral. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

We draw on published wildlife-trade regulations, conservation research, leather-industry information and the experience of leatherworkers and restorers. Regulatory information is checked against official sources and reviewed periodically.

Responsible writing

  • We present the regulations and the ethical debate around exotic leather fairly, including alternatives.
  • We never make claims about the value, resale prices or authenticity of specific items.
  • We explain technical terms in plain language.
  • We encourage buying less, caring well and repairing.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools are created by us and offer general guidance only.
