About Alligator Luxe

An independent journal for people who love fine leather goods and want to understand, choose and care for them thoughtfully.

Our story

A vintage bag and a hundred questions

Alligator Luxe began with a vintage top-handle bag found at an estate sale: deep brown, beautifully stitched and still elegant after decades. Was it alligator or crocodile? Was it legal to own? How should it be cared for?

Answering those questions meant learning about species, tanning, CITES rules and conservation, as well as craftsmanship and care. This journal shares what we learned, clearly and responsibly.

Independent

We do not sell, value or authenticate bags, and we are not affiliated with any fashion house or retailer.

Responsible

We explain the rules and the debate around exotic leather openly, including alternatives.

Craft-focused

We celebrate the skill and patience behind beautifully made leather goods.

How we work

Research and expert conversations

Our guides draw on published wildlife-trade regulations, conservation research, tannery and leather-industry information and conversations with leatherworkers and restorers. We update pages when rules or knowledge change.
